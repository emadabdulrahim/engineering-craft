# Unslop

Make the writing useful to its reader. Preserve meaning, technical precision, and the author's voice. These are editing criteria, not a word blacklist or a required sequence of passes.

## Say something concrete

- Lead with the result or the information the reader needs. Name what changes, for whom, and why when those facts matter.
- Replace inflated claims and abstract benefits with specific behavior, mechanisms, or verified measurements. Remove filler, promotional language, vague attribution, and manufactured significance.
- Use the repository's actual names for symbols, files, commands, and concepts. Keep terminology consistent instead of cycling through synonyms. Use technical terms when they are the most precise words.
- Never invent evidence, links, quotations, measurements, or verification results. Preserve uncertainty, qualifications, failure details, and security warnings. Do not make a claim sound settled merely to make it shorter.

## Make it easy to understand

- Prefer plain words and direct sentences. Split a sentence when it carries competing ideas or forces the reader to backtrack, not because it exceeds a word limit.
- Make pronouns, conditions, and scope unambiguous. Put words such as "only" and "not" next to what they modify. Keep the small words that prevent misreading.
- Write instructions in execution order. Put prerequisites and warnings before the action they govern. Distinguish an instruction from an explanation or an observed result.
- Explain constraints and consequential choices that the code cannot express. Do not narrate obvious implementation steps or repeat a title in its body.

## Keep the voice natural

- Match the audience and existing voice. Do not inject opinions, enthusiasm, first-person claims, or deliberate messiness to make text appear human.
- Remove stock openings, praise, forced contrasts, and generic conclusions. Use headings, bullets, tables, and emphasis only when they help the reader navigate real structure.
- Do not use em dashes. Otherwise, choose punctuation and sentence structure for clarity. Parentheses, passive voice, and words ending in "-ing" are not inherently problems.
- Do not churn text that is already clear. If a stylistic preference makes a sentence less accurate or harder to read, choose another edit or leave it alone.

Follow project templates and product copy guidance where applicable. Return the requested writing without an unsolicited explanation of every edit. Writing a commit message or PR description does not authorize committing or publishing it.
